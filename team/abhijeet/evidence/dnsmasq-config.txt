## A2 – dnsmasq configuration (node-1, /etc/dnsmasq.d/cn-team.conf)

```
ubuntu@node-1:~$ grep -vE '^[[:space:]]*(#|$)' /etc/dnsmasq.d/cn-team.conf
interface=ens5
listen-address=172.31.250.11
bind-interfaces
no-resolv
no-hosts
server=169.254.169.253
domain-needed
bogus-priv
local=/teamepicrouter.test/
local-ttl=30
address=/app.teamepicrouter.test/172.31.250.12
address=/api.teamepicrouter.test/172.31.250.12
address=/node-1.teamepicrouter.test/172.31.250.11
address=/node-2.teamepicrouter.test/172.31.250.12
address=/node-3.teamepicrouter.test/172.31.250.13
address=/node-4.teamepicrouter.test/172.31.250.14
log-queries
```

`listen-address` is node-1's LAN IP (not 127.0.0.1), so every machine on the LAN can use it.
`local-ttl=30` gives the answers a visible TTL of 30 s.
